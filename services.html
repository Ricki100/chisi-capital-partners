Discuss a project
01

Project Origination and Development

We identify, conceptualise and develop commercially viable opportunities, with particular interest in manufacturing, agro-processing, mineral beneficiation, import substitution, energy and other productive sectors.

Typical support

Opportunity identification; initial commercial screening; concept development; project scoping.

02

Project Preparation and Investment Structuring

We support the preparation needed to assess viability and communicate a project’s commercial logic. This may include market assessment, business planning, feasibility coordination, financial modelling, capital structuring and investment proposals.

Typical support

Project concept and business case development; financial models; capital structure options; investor-facing materials.

03

Capital Mobilisation and Transaction Advisory

We support funding strategy development and engagement with potential financiers, investors, development finance institutions and strategic partners. Our role is to prepare and facilitate discussions, not to promise financing outcomes.

Typical support

Funding strategy; potential capital-provider mapping; transaction structuring support; financing discussion coordination.

04

Fund Development and Investment Strategy

We support the development of investment strategies and fund structures oriented toward viable industrial and productive-sector opportunities, subject to applicable regulatory requirements and mandates.

Typical support

Strategy frameworks; investment mandates and criteria; structural planning with appropriate specialists.

05

Strategic Partnerships

We help bring together project originators, financial institutions, technical specialists, development finance institutions and private-sector organisations to advance viable projects.

Typical support

Partner identification; technical and commercial collaboration; stakeholder engagement.

06

What to expect

Services are scoped to the stage, complexity and needs of each opportunity. An initial discussion is not a commitment to accept a mandate, raise capital or secure financing. Commercial assessment and relevant approvals remain essential.
