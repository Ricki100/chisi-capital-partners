Chisi Capital Partners / Our approach

From an idea to a structured opportunity.

A disciplined, staged approach helps identify what a project needs before it can credibly seek investment consideration.

Start a conversation
01

Opportunity identification

We engage with project originators, enterprises and potential collaborators to understand the proposed opportunity and its context.

02

Initial commercial assessment

We examine the business rationale, potential market demand, implementation conditions and suitability for further development.

03

Project preparation

Depending on requirements, we coordinate or support business planning, feasibility inputs, modelling and project structuring.

04

Investment readiness

We help articulate the commercial proposition, financing need, proposed capital structure and materials required for informed discussions.

05

Capital-provider and partner engagement

Where appropriate, we facilitate dialogue with potential financiers, investors, technical partners and development institutions.

06

Transaction and implementation support

Subject to the mandate, we support ongoing discussions, structuring and coordination as projects move toward financing decisions and implementation.

07

Working with the Centre for Impactful Entrepreneurs

CIE focuses on outreach, entrepreneurship development, idea mobilisation and incubation. Chisi’s role begins where a suitable opportunity needs commercial development, project preparation or capital structuring. The relationship creates a potential development pathway, not an automatic transfer or guarantee of funding.

08

Important qualification

All projects remain subject to commercial assessment, due diligence, availability of relevant partners, regulatory requirements and independent financing or investment decisions.
